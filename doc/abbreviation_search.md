## 1. “Device-focused / onboarding”

Highlighting that this is a starting point for device-level interaction:
* accml-device – simple, clear, emphasizes direct device access
* accml-bridge – emphasizes that it’s a bridge from MML to accml
* accml-starter – friendly, signals onboarding / entry-level
* accml-toolkit – evokes a small collection of hands-on tools

## 2. “Lightweight / slim / familiar”

Emphasizes reduced complexity, approachable layer:
* accml-lite – common naming convention, signals reduced functionality
* accml-basic – minimal layer, entry-level functionality
* accml-mini – small, friendly, informal
* accml-core – could suggest the “core” device access without advanced abstractions

## 3. “MML-inspired / compatibility”

Signals connection to MML workflows:

* accml-mml – direct nod to MATLAB Middle Layer compatibility
* accml-mmlbridge – explicit: bridging old MML habits to accml
* accml-mmlstyle – emphasizes familiar interaction style

## 4. “Hands-on / tool-like metaphors”

Inspired by the “workbench and tools” Feynman-style metaphors we used in the text:

* accml-workbench – evokes the front bench with familiar hand tools
* accml-instrument – like a calibrated instrument for direct interaction
* accml-panel – like a control panel: close to hardware, intuitive
* accml-dial – fun, device-centric, metaphorical

## My top 3 recommendations (friendly + clear)

* accml-device – clear, concise, device-level interface, bridges MML
* accml-bridge – highlights bridging role, signals onboarding
* accml-workbench – friendly, tool-like, resonates with Feynman metaphors
